Attendees are invited to submit articles to the collection “II Latinamerican Conference on Superconductivity and Magnetism” that will be published in the Journal of Superconductivity and Novel Magnetism (Springer).

Submissions should have the form of standard research articles and will be subject to rigorous peer-review to ensure they meet the standards of quality and novelty of the journal.

 

Journal of Superconductivity and Novel Magnetism Home

The articles would be published in an special number of the journal, free of charge, unless the authors decide to publish their papers as open access. Authors can find all the information about the publication process in this link:

https://www.springer.com/journal/10948/how-to-publish-with-us

Important dates:

Date of the Event: 23rd-27th October 2023

Submission deadline of the manuscript: 31st Dec 2023

Acceptance deadline: April 30th 2024

Publication month: June 2024
